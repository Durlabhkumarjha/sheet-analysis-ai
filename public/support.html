  1. Upload your file — CSV, XLSX or XLS, up to 100,000 rows.
  2. Confirm the columns — date, revenue, product and customer columns are auto-detected; just check they're right.
  3. See your dashboard — KPIs, trends, forecast and a written summary.
  4. Ask questions in plain English, or run a full AI report.

Common questions

My code says "That code isn't valid"

Check for extra spaces when pasting, and make sure you're using the full code. If it still fails, email us with your code and where you purchased it and we'll sort it out.

I lost access after clearing my browser

Just click "Redeem code" and enter your code again — it restores your account and your remaining balance on any device.

What is an "AI action"?

One AI-powered step — a plain-English question, a chart insight, or part of a full report. Dashboards, charts, statistics and exports never use AI actions.

I've used all my AI actions

Your dashboards, charts and exports keep working. For unlimited AI, connect your own AI provider key in the app's settings — there's no extra charge from us. Paid top-up packs are coming soon.

Is my data safe?

Your file is read and analysed inside your browser and is never uploaded to our servers. See our Privacy Policy.

My numbers look wrong

Most often this is a column-mapping issue — go back to the confirm step and check the right column is selected for date and revenue. If something still looks off, email us with a description (and a sample file if you can share one) and we'll investigate.

Which files are supported?

CSV, XLSX and XLS, up to 100,000 rows. Mixed date formats, currency symbols and thousands separators are handled automatically.
