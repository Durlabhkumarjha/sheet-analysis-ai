Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

The short version: Your spreadsheet files are read and analysed inside your own browser — we never upload or store them. Optional AI features send a small, limited slice of context (column names, types and a few sample rows) to our service to generate a response.

1. What Sheet Analysis AI Is

Sheet Analysis AI is a browser-based spreadsheet analysis tool. The analysis engine — parsing, statistics, charts, KPIs and exports — runs entirely in your web browser. A small backend service (a Cloudflare Worker) supports the optional AI features and manages AI credits.

2. Your Spreadsheet Files

When you open a CSV or Excel file, it is parsed and analysed locally in your browser. The file itself is never uploaded to, or stored on, our servers. Refreshing or closing the page clears it.

3. Optional AI Features

Some features are AI-powered — plain-English questions, written summaries, recommendations, AI reports and agents. When you use one of these:

  • A limited portion of context — such as column names, column types and a few sample rows — is sent to our service and on to our AI provider (DeepSeek) solely to generate that response.
  • We do not send your full file.
  • This data is used only to produce your result. We do not use it to train models.

If you connect your own AI provider key instead, those requests go directly from your browser to that provider, and your key is stored only in your browser's localStorage — we never receive it.

4. Information We Store

To operate AI credits and lifetime licences, we store the following in Cloudflare KV:

  • An anonymous identifier generated in your browser (e.g. sai_xxxxx). It contains no name, email or personal details.
  • Your AI credit balance and usage count.
  • Licence code status, if you redeemed one, and which identifier it is linked to.

We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email address, or payment details. Purchases are handled by the marketplace you bought through — we never see your payment information.

5. What Your Browser Stores

The app uses your browser's localStorage to remember, between sessions:

  • Your anonymous identifier
  • Column mapping and report settings (title, company name, currency, brand colour)
  • AI provider/model selection and, if you use one, your own API key
  • Recent file names (not file contents)

You can clear all of this at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain.

6. Service Providers

  • Cloudflare — hosting, the backend Worker, and KV storage. May record standard technical logs (IP address, request info) for reliability and security.
  • DeepSeek — AI generation for the features described above. See platform.deepseek.com.

If you supply your own provider key, that provider's privacy policy governs those requests (e.g. OpenAI, Google, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral).

7. Cookies

We do not use cookies, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics. Local storage uses the Web Storage API, which is not transmitted with HTTP requests.

8. Data Retention

Because we don't store your files, there is nothing to delete on our side. Credit and licence records are retained while your licence remains active.

9. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any data linked to your identifier or licence code by emailing us. We will respond within a reasonable period.

10. Children's Privacy

This product is designed for business use. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. We recommend checking this page periodically.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email sheetanalysisai@gmail.com.
